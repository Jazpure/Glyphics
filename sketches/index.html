Glyphics — sketches

Rounds of visual directions for the codes.

  • Round 8 →

    The first scannable round. Black ground, primary colours only, continuous sizes, and a real encoded ID that each card reads back.

  • Round 7 →

    A collection from conservative to radical: about thirty shape kinds, bold designs, giants, exploded, fractal and shattered radiants.

  • Round 6 →

    Swell Tide, Swell Stars, Radiant Specimens and Radiant Bloom mixed, across a range of drawing styles.

  • Round 5 →

    Denser Swell, cohesive Weave, airier Petri, and Radiant: bead lines converging on the centre of the whole oval.

  • Round 4 →

    Strands of multicoloured shapes crossed with Petri, Specimen Reef, Nested Cells and Colonies, plus denser foams.

  • Round 3 →

    Four sets built from Bubble Reef and Branching Spokes (Reef, Genome, Type, Canopy), random rather than radial, in black and white or full colour.

  • Round 2 →

    Twelve hybrids built on growth and layered depth. Three colors, even density, a shared centre and rim.

  • Round 1 →

    Twelve first directions, from calligraphy to atmosphere.
