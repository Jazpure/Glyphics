Glyphics — round 6

Swell Tide, Swell Stars, Radiant Specimens and Radiant Bloom mixed into six compositions, each drawn in a different style: flat, line, woodcut, collage, riso, blueprint, cut paper, shadow, halftone, pen and stipple. The switch redraws every design in pure black and white, full colour, or a random mix.
